About MediMike

Intelligent AI Medical Record Management & Analysis System

What is MediMike?

MediMike is an intelligent medical record management system that uses artificial intelligence to digitize, organize, and analyze medical reports, translating complex clinical jargon into accessible, structured records and summaries for healthcare professionals and patients.

Features

  • Patient demographic & clinical profile management
  • Medical report upload (PDF documents & image scans)
  • Automated OCR and text extraction engine
  • AI-powered clinical information extraction (vitals, lab metrics, diagnoses, prescriptions)
  • Plain-language, patient-friendly AI summaries
  • Side-by-side verification with original reports
  • Secure SQLite database persistence

Medical Safety Disclaimer

IMPORTANT: This application is designed for information processing and record organization only.

  • AI-generated information is provided for record organization and informational purposes only
  • It does NOT replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment
  • All extracted information should be verified against the original report
  • MediMike AI does NOT diagnose patients independently
  • MediMike AI does NOT prescribe medication or recommend treatment
  • Users should always consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical decisions
